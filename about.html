About Vigilant Marine Systems

Practical innovation for a demanding marine industry.

Part of the Vigilant Marine Group, Vigilant Marine Systems was established to manufacture, market and distribute products and systems designed within the group.

Our story

A marine-industry focus built over time.

The Vigilant Marine Group has been servicing the marine industry since 1993. Vigilant Marine Systems was established in 2001.

The company’s stated focus is to provide the commercial marine industry with innovative, reliable and cost-effective products and systems designed for demanding marine conditions.

Vigilant also describes cost-effectiveness as more than a low initial purchase price: products should last, be serviceable by competent crew members and minimise installation costs.
